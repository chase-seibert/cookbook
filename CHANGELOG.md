# Changelog

## 2026-10-21

- Added a SwiftUI iOS app foundation with offline recipe browsing, list and photo-grid modes, recipe detail, cooking mode, Reminders review, notes, photo attachments, print/PDF sharing, Settings, and saved dinner drafts.
- Added a deterministic LaTeX catalog generator and bundled 75 recipe entries from 67 indexed source files.
- Added iOS simulator and physical-device Makefile workflows and mobile project documentation.
- Fixed catalog parsing so instruction paragraphs are retained, cookbook chapter order is preserved, and recipe subsections stay attached to their parent.
- Cocktails, Adobo, Plums, and Salad Dressings now appear as collection headings with their individual recipes; Carving and Secret Sauce remain parts of their parent recipes.
- Tightened ingredient spacing and grocery labels, excluded plain water, expanded pantry staples, exposed Start Cooking, and added date-based dinners with a separate Saved dinners section.
