# Salad Dressings

## Phil's Famous

> **Introduction:** My father used to make this dressing for salads and marinades at almost every family gathering, as well as once a week for regular dinners.

| Quantity | Ingredient |
|---|---|
| 3 | tablespoons olive oil |
| 2 | tablespoons vinegar |
| 1 | tablespoon Grey Poupon |
| 1 | teaspoon oregano |
| 1 | garlic clove |

Combine ingredients in a small jar, and shake vigorously. The emulsion is stable for at least an hour.

## Lemon Vinaigrette

> **Introduction:** This simple dressing is from Pam and Rick. I remember them serving it many times in Bar Harbor. It's great with just mixed greens, or with cucumber.

| Quantity | Ingredient |
|---|---|
| 1 | garlic clove |

Cut clove in half and rub the against the inside of a wooden salad bowl. Discard the clove.

| Quantity | Ingredient |
|---|---|
| 3 | tablespoons olive oil |
| 2 | tablespoons lemon juice |
| 1 | teaspoon kosher salt |

Combine ingredients in the bottom of the bowl, whisk to combine, and toss salad.

## Parmesan Peppercorn

| Quantity | Ingredient |
|---|---|
| ¾ | cup mayonnaise |
| ¼ | cup sour cream |
| 3 | tablespoons Parmesan |
| 2 | tablespoons milk |
| 2 | tablespoons vinegar |
| 1 | tablespoon black pepper |
| 1 | teaspoon garlic powder |
| 1 | teaspoon basil |

Whisk together in a medium bowl, and refrigerate for at least an hour.
