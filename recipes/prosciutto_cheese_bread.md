# Prosciutto Cheese Bread

> **Introduction:** Great side dish for a pork roast.

1. Pre-heat the oven to 400°.

| Quantity | Ingredient |
|---|---|
| 1 | package active dry yeast |
| 1 | cup warm water |
| 1 | teaspoon sugar |

1. Combine in the bowl of a stand mixer. Let it sit for 8 minutes.

| Quantity | Ingredient |
|---|---|
| 1 | tablespoon olive oil |
| 2 | cups flour |
| 1 | teaspoon kosher salt |
| 1 | tablespoon dried herbs |

1. Combine using a dough hook in the stand mixer for 5 minutes. If it's still sticky, add flour as needed.

1. Cover and let rest for 90 minutes.

1. Punch down dough. Flatten into an oiled baking sheet.

| Quantity | Ingredient |
|---|---|
| ½ | cut Prosciutto |
| ½ | cut provolone cheese |
| ½ | cut Parmesan cheese |

1. Spread ingredients onto the dough. Roll up into a cylinder, and then seal the ends together. Cover with plastic and let rise again, 60 minutes.

| Quantity | Ingredient |
|---|---|
| 1 | egg |

1. Brush with egg wash. Bake for 25 minutes until golden brown.
