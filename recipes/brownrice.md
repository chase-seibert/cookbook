# Brown Rice Salad

> **Introduction:** Brown rice does not need to be boiled in a precise amount of water like white rice. You can cook it like pasta.

| Quantity | Ingredient |
|---|---|
| 2 | cups brown rice |

1. Boil in a large amount of water for 30 or minutes. It should be al-dente.

| Quantity | Ingredient |
|---|---|
| 1 | bunch parsley |
| 1 | bunch cilantro |
| 1 | bunch mint |
| ½ | cup olive oil |
| 3 | teaspoons red wine vinegar |
| 8 | ounces feta cheese |

1. Mince herbs and combine with cooled drained rice.

> **Tip:** Variations include adding sliced cherry tomatoes, cucumbers or preserved lemon.
