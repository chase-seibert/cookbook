# Thai Cashew Chicken (Gai Pad Med Ma Muang)

> **Introduction:** Serve with Jasmine rice.

| Quantity | Ingredient |
|---|---|
| 1 | tablespoon soy sauce |
| 2 | tablespoons flour |
| 1½ | lbs chicken thighs |
|  | salt |
|  | pepper |

1. Combine soy sauce and flour with a whisk. Slice chicken to ¾ inch cubes and toss in sauce.

1. Put a wok or cast iron pan over high heat. Don't preheat for more than a few minutes or the first oil could burn.

| Quantity | Ingredient |
|---|---|
| 5 | tablespoons vegetable oil |

1. Stir fry each batch staring with 1 tablespoon of oil.

1. Stir fry chicken in two batches. Remove to temporary bowl.

| Quantity | Ingredient |
|---|---|
| 1 | cup roasted cashews |

1. Stir fry cashews. Remove to bowl.

| Quantity | Ingredient |
|---|---|
| 5 | Arbol chiles |
| 1 | teaspoon red pepper flakes |
| 6 | garlic cloves |

1. Press garlic into a mince and combine ingredients into a small bowl.

| Quantity | Ingredient |
|---|---|
| 1 | red onion |

1. Slice onion into one inch chunks.

1. Stir fry garlic, red pepper. Add onions. Cook for 2 minutes.

| Quantity | Ingredient |
|---|---|
| 2 | tablespoons soy sauce |
| 1½ | tablespoons Thai Kitchen roasted red chili paste |
| 1½ | tablespoons sugar |
| 1 | tablespoon oyster sauce |
| 3 | tablespoons water |

1. Whisk in a bowl.

1. Add chicken back to pan. Add roasted red pepper sauce. Cook for 2 minutes.

| Quantity | Ingredient |
|---|---|
| 1 | bunch scallions |

1. Slice green onions into one inch pieces and add to pan. Cook for 1 minute.
