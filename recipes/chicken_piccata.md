# Chicken Piccata

| Quantity | Ingredient |
|---|---|
| 2 | boneless skinless chicken breasts |
|  | kosher salt |

1. Slice chicken breasts in half horizontally and season with salt. Pound them flat. Let rest for 1 hour.

| Quantity | Ingredient |
|---|---|
| 1 | cup flour |
| 1 | tablespoon vegatable oil |

1. Dry chicken with paper towels and dredge lightly in flour.

1. Oil a saute pan and brown chicken on both sides over medium heat. Remove to a plate.

| Quantity | Ingredient |
|---|---|
| 1 | shallot |
| 1 | cup chicken stock |
| 2 | lemons |
| 2 | tablespoons capers |

1. Mince shallot and saute in empty pan with salt. Add chicken stock to de-glaze, along with one sliced lemon.

1. Reduce, and then add the juice of two lemons plus the capers. Reduce again.

| Quantity | Ingredient |
|---|---|
| 3 | tablespoons butter |
|  | parsley |

1. Whisk in the cold butter one tablespoon at a time. Sauce the chicken and top with parsley.
