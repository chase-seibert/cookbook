# Anne's Spinach Salad

> **Introduction:** This recipe is from my Aunt Anne, who swears that she always used romaine lettuce instead of spinach.

| Quantity | Ingredient |
|---|---|
| 2 | eggs |

1. Boil eggs, cool, shell and slice into rings.

| Quantity | Ingredient |
|---|---|
| 10 | strips bacon |

1. Separate strips onto aluminum foil on a baking sheet, and bake for 15 minutes at 425°. Pat dry and chop into small pieces.

| Quantity | Ingredient |
|---|---|
| ½ | cup olive oil |
| ¾ | can anchovies in oil |
| 2 | tablespoons balsamic |
| 2 | tablespoons lemon juice |
| 1 | garlic clove |
| ½ | teaspoon thyme |
| ¼ | teaspoon |
| ¼ | sugar |
| ¼ | oregano |
| ¼ | mustard powder |
| ¼ | onion salt |
| ¼ | paprika |

1. Discard the oil in the can and use only the anchovies. Liquefy all ingredients in a blender.

| Quantity | Ingredient |
|---|---|
| 1 | package spinach |
|  | pre-sliced mushrooms |

1. Combine eggs, bacon and dressing in a large mixing bowl.

> **Tip:** The dressing can keep at least overnight.

> **Tip:** To shell eggs easily, run under cold water, shaking pan to smash shells. The water will seep in. In 10 minutes, the shells will come off easily.
